# GEX Lab candidate universe.
#
# Seed list of commonly optionable names. The weekly scan measures each one's
# real liquidity from its own chain and keeps only what clears the thresholds
# in config.toml, so an illiquid name here costs you one fetch, not a bad level.
#
# Edit freely. Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
#
# Two known gotchas:
#   - Berkshire is listed as BRKB, not BRK.B — options roots drop the dot.
#   - This list reflects index membership as of authoring. Mergers, delistings
#     and ticker changes happen; the scan reports every candidate it could not
#     fetch as a named list rather than swallowing it, so prune from that.
