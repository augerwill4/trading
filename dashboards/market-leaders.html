What every one of these had in common

Ten years, ten cycles, roughly two hundred names. The themes never repeat exactly, but the anatomy does. This is the part worth memorizing — the list above is just evidence for it.

A new demand curve, not a cheap valuation

Not one name on this list led because it was undervalued. Each one sat in front of demand that did not exist two years earlier: GPUs for training, HBM for inference, gigawatts for data centers, NAND for storage, uranium for reactors, magnets outside China.

Screening for cheap gets you 2022 energy. Screening for new demand gets you the other nine cycles.

Earnings acceleration separates the long runs from the fast ones

NVDA, SMCI, MU, APP, PLTR, VST and CVNA all delivered accelerating revenue and EPS while the stock ran. Those moves lasted quarters to years and gave you multiple entries.

QBTS, LFIN, DWAC, SBET, GME and the 2018 cannabis complex had no earnings at all. Those moves lasted days to weeks and gave you one entry with no second chance.

Small float and short interest buy velocity, not durability

Float is a multiplier on whatever the fundamentals already are. TLRY in 2018, GME in 2021, BYND in 2019 and the 2025 crypto-treasury names were float mechanics with a story attached — they went vertical and gave it all back.

Tight float + real earnings is the rarest and best combination on this page: SMCI 2023, APP 2024, CRDO 2024.

Somebody real validates the theme first

The move usually starts on an outside stamp, not a press release. Constellation's stake in CGC. Nvidia's first guidance raise in May 2023. The DoD taking equity in MP in July 2025. Cipher's 15-year AWS lease. Oracle's RPO disclosure. AMD's OpenAI agreement.

Watch for the counterparty, not the company.

Leaders travel in groups of three to eight

Memory was never just MU — it was MU, WDC, STX, SNDK. Power was never just VST — it was VST, CEG, TLN, NRG, GEV, BE. Quantum was IONQ, RGTI, QBTS, QUBT.

If you cannot name three, you do not have a theme. You have one stock and a story.

Sectors lead more than once — and rhyme when they do

Memory led 2016–17, again in 2024, and is leading the tape right now in 2026. Solar led 2019–20 and again in 2022. Nuclear led 2024 and 2025. Crypto proxies led Q4 2017, Q1 and Q4 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025.

Every prior leg is a free template for the next one. Study the 2016 Micron chart before you trade the 2026 one.

The endings look identical

Acceleration into a near-vertical slope. An exhaustion gap on the highest volume of the entire move. A wide-range reversal bar. Then the first weekly close below the 21-week line that does not get bought back.

SMCI in March 2024, TLRY in September 2018, LFIN in December 2017, PTON in January 2021, ENPH in 2021. Same shape every time.

The last name up is the tell

When the junk in a group starts outrunning the leader, the cycle is late. LFIN going 26x with no business marked the top of the 2017 crypto move. NKLA and the 2020 SPACs marked the top of the EV move. The 2025 crypto-treasury shells marked the top of that trade.

Ask what quality of name is making the biggest move today. That answer dates the cycle better than any indicator.

  • Percentages are approximate and rounded. They describe the size of the leadership move over the window shown — sometimes a calendar year, sometimes a base-to-peak run that crosses years. Treat them as magnitude buckets, not as data you would backtest on. Pull the actual chart before you conclude anything.
  • 2024, 2025 and 2026 figures are the most reliable — those were checked against published year-end and year-to-date performance rankings. Figures from 2016–2023 come from the historical record and carry more rounding error.
  • “Liquid” here means it traded with real institutional participation at the time of the move. A handful of names were small caps that became liquid because of the move (TSSI, ONDS, LFIN). Those are marked in the notes and should be studied as float mechanics, not as investments.
  • Survivorship is deliberate. This is a list of what led, which means it is also a list of names that mostly gave large portions of it back. LFIN was delisted. SMCI fell about 70% from its 2024 high. PTON, ENPH, UPST and most of the 2021 complex are down 80–95% from their peaks. Leadership is a phase, not an identity.
